# Five Seconds to Explain the Glass

The house cat's defence, delivered five seconds after the glass left the table: the glass had occupied the cat's spot for three hours without once apologising, so the cat evicted it. No apology from the cat, no confession — just territorial logic.

## Entry

`main.py` writes one sentence (85 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> That glass sat in my spot for three hours and never once apologised, so I evicted it.

The joke turns the blame around: the glass is the rude one, the cat is merely enforcing the house rules. Standard library only, no network, no third-party packages, deterministic.
